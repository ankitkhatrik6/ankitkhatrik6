## `[ 01 ]` · whoami

Full stack developer out of Kathmandu, currently second semester into a CSIT degree at ASCOL. No fixed lane — some civic tech, some fintech-shaped experiments, some purely *"does this idea even work"* builds. The thread connecting all of it: most of what I ship starts as a problem I personally ran into, usually something Nepal- or TU-specific that nobody had bothered to fix properly.

I care more about *why* something broke than the fact that it did. That's the part pulling me toward AI/ML and security these days — not as buzzwords, but as the next layer of *"how does this actually work."*

## `[ 02 ]` · the stack

Most nights I'm writing **TypeScript** — it's where I live now. **Python** shows up when something needs to be quick or data-shaped, and everything else is just whatever the problem demands.
