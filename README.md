## ๑ `[ 01 ]` · whoami ๑

<img align="right" width="205" alt="totoro~" src="https://media.giphy.com/media/jsOU42Wmd7Vfy/giphy.gif"/>

Full stack developer from Kathmandu, currently in my second semester of CSIT at ASCOL ♡

I don't really have a fixed lane. I build civic tech stuff, fintech-ish experiments, random tools, and a lot of *“does this even work?”* projects.

Most of the things I make start from a problem I personally had, usually something Nepal or TU related that was annoying enough that I thought *“fine, I'll just build it myself.”*

I care more about why something broke than just fixing the thing and moving on. That's also why I've been getting more into AI/ML and security lately. Not because they sound cool, but because I wanna understand what's actually happening under the hood.

Still figuring things out, still building random stuff, and probably gonna keep doing that. <br>(｡•̀ᴗ-)✧
